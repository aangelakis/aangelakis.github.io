I hold an MSc in Computer Science and Engineering from the [University of Crete](https://www.csd.uoc.gr) (grade: **9.64/10.00**), specializing in **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Signal Processing**. My thesis, **Adaptive AM-FM Features for Parkinson's Disease Detection from Speech**, was supervised by Dr. George Kafentzis in the [Speech Signal Processing Lab](https://www.csd.uoc.gr/~sspl/) (SSPL), where I have been a research assistant since 2024. My work there also includes cough-based tuberculosis detection and contributing to [STOMA](https://huggingface.co/datasets/aangelakis/STOMA), a multi-speaker Greek speech corpus, on which I trained TTS architectures (FastSpeech2, VITS, Tacotron2, HiFi-GAN). During my Master's studies, I also served as a Teaching Assistant for *Applied Mathematics for Engineers*, *Digital Signal Processing*, and *Digital Speech Signal Processing*, supporting lectures, grading, and student mentoring.

From 2022 to 2026, I was also a research assistant at the [Data Science Lab of IACM-FORTH](https://www.iacm.forth.gr/divisions/numerical-analysis-computational-systems/data-science), working with Dr. Yannis Pantazis. There I was the lead developer of NeuroDiMe, a Python library for neural-based statistical distance estimation (f-divergences, IPMs) supporting PyTorch, JAX, and TensorFlow, and I developed graph traversal algorithms for sensor placement and leak localization in water distribution networks.

I also hold a BSc in Computer Science from the University of Crete (GPA: **8.77/10.00**), where I worked as a teaching assistant for courses including *Applied Mathematics for Engineers*, *Probabilities*, and *Information System Analysis and Design*. I have received multiple academic honors, including **four scholarships from the State Scholarships Foundation (IKY)** (2020–2023) and the **Chrysanthos and Anastasia Karidis Scholarship** for outstanding performance in the national entrance exams.

I have actively engaged in the academic community by delivering outreach presentations to high school students, highlighting research from the Speech Signal Processing Laboratory, and mentoring first-year students through the **STEER mentoring program** as a Peer Mentor.
